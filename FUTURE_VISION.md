## 🚀 PHASE 1: DEPTH & IMMERSION

### 1. Dynamic Personality System
**Concept:** Player develops personality traits that affect all decisions

**Traits (Develop Over Time):**
- **Risk Tolerance** (0-100) - Affects investment choices, business decisions
- **Work Ethic** (0-100) - Affects career progression, burnout risk
- **Social Skills** (0-100) - Affects networking, relationship success
- **Creativity** (0-100) - Affects business innovation, problem-solving
- **Discipline** (0-100) - Affects study success, debt management

**Trait Development:**
- Choices shape personality over time
- "You took 5 risky investments → Risk Tolerance +10"
- "You worked 60-hour weeks for 6 months → Work Ethic +5, Happiness -20"
- Traits unlock unique opportunities
- High Creativity → Invent new product line
- High Social Skills → Network into better job

**Impact:**
- Personality-based random events
- Career paths that match personality
- Business strategies aligned with traits
- Relationship compatibility based on traits

### 2. Mentor & Networking System
**Concept:** Build relationships that provide guidance and opportunities

**Mentor Types:**
- **Career Mentor** - Advice on promotions, job changes
- **Business Mentor** - Strategic guidance, connections
- **Financial Advisor** - Investment tips, tax strategies
- **Life Coach** - Work-life balance, happiness management

**Networking Mechanics:**
- Attend events (cost time + money)
- Build relationship scores (0-100)
- Unlock opportunities through connections
- "Your mentor introduced you to a VC → Business loan at 6% instead of 8%"
- "Your network helped you land a job → Skip interview, instant hire"

**Relationship Decay:**
- Must maintain relationships (monthly check-ins)
- Neglected relationships fade
- Can lose mentors if you ignore advice

### 4. Life Crisis Events
**Concept:** Major life events that test player's resilience

**Crisis Types:**

**Health Crises:**
- Serious illness (medical bills, time off work)
- Mental health episode (reduced productivity)
- Injury (temporary disability)
- **Player Choice:** Pay for treatment vs tough it out
- **Long-term Impact:** Affects energy, happiness, insurance needs

**Family Crises:**
- Parent needs care (time + money commitment)
- Divorce (asset split, emotional toll)
- Child illness (medical costs, stress)
- **Player Choice:** Family first vs career first
- **Consequences:** Relationship changes, financial impact

**Career Crises:**
- Company bankruptcy (sudden unemployment)
- Industry disruption (skills obsolete)
- Workplace scandal (reputation damage)
- **Player Choice:** Pivot vs double down
- **Recovery Path:** Retraining, networking, entrepreneurship

**Financial Crises:**
- Identity theft (credit score damage)
- Investment fraud (lose portion of portfolio)
- Lawsuit (legal fees, potential loss)
- **Player Choice:** Fight vs settle
- **Insurance:** Can mitigate with proper coverage
